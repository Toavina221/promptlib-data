Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Overview

Welcome to PromptLib! Our app gives you access to a curated gallery of AI image prompts and a Workspace where you can test them out on your own photos. We want you to create with complete peace of mind: PromptLib does not use user accounts and contains no tracking or analytics SDKs. The only external service we rely on is Google AdMob to show ads (more details below).

Photos you edit

When you hit Generate, your photo is first resized locally on your device (keeping the longest edge at 512 px) so it travels quickly and securely over HTTPS alongside your prompt text. Our server immediately forwards everything to Cloudflare Workers AI (powered by the FLUX.2 [klein] model from Black Forest Labs) to create your edited image, and sends it straight back to your device. Rest assured: our server never saves your photo, your prompt, or the final result. Cloudflare handles these requests strictly under its own terms and privacy policy.

Results

Your newly created image lives temporarily in the app's cache. It will only ever be written to your device gallery if you explicitly tap Save, and it leaves your device only if you choose to share it.

Advertising

To keep PromptLib free and accessible for everyone, we display banner ads, full-screen ads, and rewarded videos via Google AdMob. To serve and measure these ads properly, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, and standard usage information, and may personalize ads where permitted. Whenever required by local laws (such as in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland), the app will present a consent form when you first open it. You can update your choices at any time directly from the menu at the top of the Gallery (under Ad privacy choices) or via your device settings. For more information on how Google manages this data, please check out policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Data stored on your device

To give you a smooth experience, the app stores a few lightweight items locally on your phone: your daily generation counter (along with any extra bonuses earned from rewarded videos), a pacing counter for full-screen ads, a cached copy of the prompt gallery so you can browse offline, timestamps for your unlocked prompts (each unlock lasts 24 hours), and a local history log containing small thumbnails of your successful generations, the prompts used, and dates. This history stays strictly on your device, is never uploaded anywhere, and can be cleared or managed item-by-item directly from the History screen. Nothing else is kept behind.

Gallery content

Our prompt gallery is fetched dynamically from a public GitHub repository. Whenever the app loads or refreshes the gallery, GitHub naturally receives your IP address and standard request data.

Contributor submissions

If you choose to use our contributor feature to submit a new prompt, the image, title, category, tags, and text you provide are uploaded for review. If approved, they will be published publicly in that same GitHub repository. Please make sure not to submit anything you wouldn't want to be public!

Permissions

We only ask for device permissions when you need them: Camera access is used only when you tap the Camera button; photo selection uses the system picker so PromptLib only sees the single image you explicitly choose; and gallery write access is requested only when you tap Save.

Children

PromptLib is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any data from them.

Changes and contact

We may occasionally update this policy to reflect new features or changes. The date at the top always shows the latest version. If you have any questions, we'd love to hear from you at Toavina.supportclient@gmail.com.
